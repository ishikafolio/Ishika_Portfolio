## Anime hero character

Source: `Anime Character Sheet_ Confident Creative Woman.png`, supplied in the workspace.

Website assets: `public/assets/anime-character-sheet.png` (unchanged source sheet for the expression card and About and Contact cameos) and `public/assets/anime-character-cutout.png` (transparent hero cutout). The original cutout is archived at `documentation/original-assets/anime-character-cutout.png`. The cutout was edited with the built-in image generation tool from the sheet. Expression crops, pose changes, and animation are implemented in CSS and JavaScript.

### Cutout prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Isolate the smiling three-quarter-view woman from the supplied sheet, preserving her glasses, wavy black hair, dark sleeveless top, belt, white trousers, facial features, proportions, and illustration style. Frame from hair to upper thighs, remove all page elements and background, and output genuine transparency with clean edges and no glow or text.

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The supplied screenshots were used for visual direction; their artwork and the reference designer’s portrait were not copied into the website. The current visual direction follows `inspiration/Inspiration_2`.

## Botanical hero

Website asset: `public/assets/botanical-hero.webp`

Original: `documentation/original-assets/botanical-hero.png`

Archived from the first design direction. The current site uses CSS shapes and typography in these sections.

### Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a cinematic botanical background for a graphic designer's portfolio website. Generate an original landscape 1536x1024 fine art still life: deep oxblood burgundy red matte studio wall with delicate dark botanical shadows in upper corners. At the bottom, lush sculptural arrangement of tall iridescent periwinkle and plum tropical leaves, coral pink bird of paradise flowers, fluffy mauve hydrangeas, small yellow green blooms and thin airy golden branches. Flowers cluster in the bottom third with the tallest purple leaves reaching up at right and left. Center and upper 60 percent of the image are mostly empty burgundy negative space for website typography. Rich dramatic sidelight, dark editorial photographic treatment, tactile paper-like subtle grain, premium art direction, strong chiaroscuro, realistic elegant botanical photography. No people, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks.

## Aara tea identity mockup

Website asset: `public/assets/aara-brand.webp`

Original: `documentation/original-assets/aara-brand.png`

A fictional brand concept with AI-assisted packaging imagery, identified as a concept mockup in its case study.

### Generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: portfolio concept project visual for a fictional premium Indian tea brand named AARA. Create a beautiful editorial product still-life landscape photo 1536x1024. On a muted sage olive studio floor and backdrop, arrange two tall rectangular tea packaging boxes, one cream and one deep forest green, a small olive green cylindrical tea tin, a cream business card and a soft cream sheet of stationery. Packaging typography is a very large elegant high contrast serif wordmark 'aara' with small letterspaced 'TEA & STILLNESS' beneath, delicate thin botanical tea branch line illustrations and tiny terracotta accent details. One box slightly taller in back, foreground tin and business card. Include a single fresh tea branch lying gently on floor. Warm diagonal afternoon sunlight creates long elegant soft shadows. Color palette olive, cream, forest green, muted terracotta. Sophisticated minimal typography, premium real paper textures, perfectly art directed commercial photography. No other brand names, no watermark.
